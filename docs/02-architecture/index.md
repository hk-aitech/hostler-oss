## Conventions

- ADRs follow the [Michael Nygard format](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions).
- Filenames: `ADR-NNN-<kebab-title>.md`.
- Status: `Proposed`, `Accepted`, `Deprecated`, `Superseded by ADR-NNN`.
- An accepted ADR is immutable — supersede it with a new one rather than editing in place.
